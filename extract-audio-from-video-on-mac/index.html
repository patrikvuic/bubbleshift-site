How to extract the audio from a video on a Mac

Want only the sound of a recording, lecture or interview? Here is how to save the audio of a video as its own file on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the video in Finder.
  2. Tap ⇧ Shift while dragging and drop on M4A.
  3. The audio file appears beside the video.
Coming soon to the Mac App Store€4.99 once · no subscription · works offline

With what macOS already has

QuickTime Player can do it: open the video and choose File → Export As → Audio Only.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

It works straight from Finder for any number of videos. M4A plays on iPhone, Mac, Windows, Android and in browsers.

Questions

Why is there no M4A option for my video?

Bubbleshift only offers it when the video has sound; screen recordings often don't.

Can I get an MP3?

macOS has no MP3 encoder, so Bubbleshift saves M4A, which plays nearly everywhere MP3 does.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The audio is extracted on your Mac.
