How to resize images in bulk on a Mac

Websites, shops and emails often need smaller photos. Here is how to resize one image or hundreds at once on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Select the images in Finder and start dragging.
  2. Tap ⌘ Command while dragging and rest on Resize for a moment.
  3. Drop on a size: 50%, or a longest side of 1080, 2048 or 3840 pixels. Resized copies appear beside the originals.

With what macOS already has

In Preview, select the images in the sidebar, choose Tools → Adjust Size and save. It changes the originals unless you export copies.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

Your originals stay untouched, each photo keeps its format, orientation and metadata, and a whole selection is done in one gesture.

Questions

Will resizing change my original photos?

No. Bubbleshift saves resized copies beside them.

Can I choose an exact size?

Yes. Drop on Custom and drag the image's corner, or set an exact width and height in the queue.

Does it keep the photo's orientation?

Yes. Portrait photos stay upright.
