How to open RAR and 7Z files on a Mac

Finder opens ZIP files with a double-click, but RAR and 7Z archives, common on Windows, usually need an extra app. Here is how to extract them, and how to zip files to send.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the RAR, 7Z, TAR.GZ or other archive in Finder.
  2. Tap ⇧ Shift while dragging and drop on Extract.
  3. The contents appear in a folder beside the archive. An archive that holds a single folder unpacks to just that folder.

With what macOS already has

For ZIP files, double-click them in Finder. Many versions of macOS can't open RAR or 7Z on their own.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

One gesture handles ZIP, RAR, 7Z, TAR, TAR.GZ, TGZ, BZ2 and GZ. It works the other way too: drag a folder or several files, tap Shift and drop on ZIP to get one archive that opens cleanly on any computer.

Questions

Can it open password-protected archives?

Not yet. Password-protected archives aren't supported.

Will the ZIP files open on Windows?

Yes. Archives are made without Mac-only metadata files, so they open cleanly on Windows.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Extracting and zipping happen on your Mac.
