How to make a photo collage on a Mac

Before-and-after shots, a few holiday photos for one post, screenshots side by side for a bug report: here is how to combine photos into one image on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Select two to twelve photos in Finder and start dragging them.
  2. Tap ⌘ Command while dragging and drop on Collage.
  3. Pick Side by side, Stacked or Grid, and drag a photo onto another to swap them. Or pick Free: drag photos anywhere, edges snap to each other, and the corner resizes.
  4. Choose a transparent, white or black background and click Make collage. The new image appears beside the first photo.

With what macOS already has

macOS has no collage tool. You can place photos on a blank Keynote slide or Pages document, arrange them by hand and export the slide as an image (in Keynote: File → Export To → Images).

Why use Bubbleshift for this

It starts from a Finder drag, lays photos out in one click, and saves the collage at the resolution of your sharpest photo (up to 8192 pixels on the longest side). Nothing is uploaded.

Questions

How many photos can a collage have?

Two to twelve.

Which format is the collage saved in?

PNG, which keeps a transparent background. With a white or black background and only JPEG or HEIC photos, it's saved as JPEG.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The collage is made on your Mac.
