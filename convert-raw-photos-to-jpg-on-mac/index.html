How to convert RAW photos to JPG on a Mac

Camera RAW files are large and can't be shared or posted directly. Here is how to turn them into JPGs, or HEIC, PNG or TIFF, on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Select the RAW files in Finder and start dragging.
  2. Tap ⇧ Shift while dragging and drop on JPG.
  3. The JPGs appear beside the RAW files, the right way up. The RAW originals are untouched.

With what macOS already has

Open the RAW file in Preview and choose File → Export, or import it into Photos and export it from there.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

It converts a whole selection from a single drag, supports the RAW formats of Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm, Olympus, Panasonic, Pentax, Samsung and DNG, and you can resize them for the web in the same moment with the Resize action.

Questions

Which RAW formats are supported?

Those macOS can read, including Canon CR2 and CR3, Nikon NEF, Sony ARW, Fujifilm RAF, Olympus ORF, Panasonic RW2, Pentax PEF, Samsung SRW and DNG.

Does it apply my editing?

No. It converts the RAW image as macOS develops it by default; edits made in other apps aren't included.

Can I make smaller JPGs for the web?

Yes. Use the ⌘ Command ring and rest on Resize to pick 1080, 2048 or 3840 pixels.
