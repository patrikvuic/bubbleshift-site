How to convert a PDF to Word on a Mac

Sometimes you need to edit the text in a PDF. Here is how to turn it into a Word document on your Mac, without sending it to an online converter.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the PDF in Finder.
  2. Tap ⇧ Shift while dragging and drop on DOCX.
  3. The Word document appears beside the PDF.

With what macOS already has

Open the PDF in Preview, select the text and copy it into Pages or Word. Formatting is lost and it's slow for long documents.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

It keeps the text with its fonts and sizes, one Word page per PDF page, and your document stays on your Mac. To be honest about the limits: images, tables and exact positioning are not rebuilt.

Questions

Will the Word file look exactly like the PDF?

The text, fonts and sizes are kept; images, tables and exact layout are not.

What about scanned PDFs?

Scans have no text to convert. Use the Text action to read the text from them first.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. It is converted on your Mac.
