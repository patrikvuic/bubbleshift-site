How to remove a photo's background on a Mac

Product photos, profile pictures and stickers often need a transparent background. Here is how to cut out the subject on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the photo in Finder.
  2. Tap ⌘ Command while dragging and drop on Cutout.
  3. A PNG with a transparent background appears beside the photo.
Coming soon to the Mac App Store€4.99 once · no subscription · works offline

With what macOS already has

In Preview, open the image and choose Tools → Remove Background (macOS 14 or later), then save a copy.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

It's one drag from Finder, works on several photos at once, and runs on your Mac, so private photos aren't uploaded. Requires macOS 14 or later.

Questions

Which photos work best?

Photos with a clear main subject, such as a person, pet or product.

What format is the result?

A PNG, which keeps the transparency.

Is the photo uploaded?

No. Background removal runs on your Mac.
