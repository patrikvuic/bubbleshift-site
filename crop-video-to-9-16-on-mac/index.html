How to crop a video to vertical 9:16 on a Mac

Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts want vertical video. Here is how to crop a landscape clip to 9:16, or any other shape, on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the video in Finder.
  2. Tap ⌘ Command while dragging and drop on Crop.
  3. Choose 9:16 (or 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, or Free), move the frame over the part you want to keep, then click Crop.

With what macOS already has
