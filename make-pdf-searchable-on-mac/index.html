How to make a scanned PDF searchable on a Mac

Scans and photographed documents are just pictures: you can't search or copy their text. Here is how to fix that on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the scanned PDF (or a photo of a document) in Finder.
  2. Tap ⌘ Command while dragging and drop on Searchable.
  3. A searchable copy appears beside the original. PDFs that already have text are left alone.

With what macOS already has

Live Text in Preview lets you select text in a scan while you look at it, but it isn't saved into the PDF, so Spotlight and other apps still can't find it.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

The recognized text is saved into the PDF, invisibly, over the original pages, so the file looks identical but Spotlight, Preview and any PDF reader can search it.

Questions

Does the PDF look different?

No. The text layer is invisible; the scan is untouched.

Which languages work?

The languages macOS's text recognition supports, detected automatically.

Is my document uploaded?

No. Text recognition runs on your Mac.
