How to convert WebP images to JPG on a Mac

Images saved from websites are often WebP, which some apps and upload forms reject. Here is how to turn them into JPG or PNG on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Select the WebP images in Finder and start dragging.
  2. Tap ⇧ Shift while dragging and drop on JPG (or PNG to keep transparency).
  3. The converted copies appear beside the originals.

With what macOS already has

Open the WebP image in Preview and choose File → Export, then JPEG or PNG. That is one image at a time.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

It converts any number of images in one drag and keeps transparency when you choose PNG.

Questions

Should I choose JPG or PNG?

JPG for photos (smaller files), PNG for graphics, logos and anything with transparency.

Can Bubbleshift save WebP?

Not yet: it reads WebP but saves JPG, PNG, HEIC, AVIF and other formats.

Are the images uploaded?

No. Conversion runs on your Mac.
