How to remove location data from photos on a Mac

Most phone photos record exactly where they were taken. Before posting or sending a photo, you may want to remove that location, along with other hidden details.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the photo (or several) in Finder.
  2. Tap ⌘ Command while dragging and drop on Clean.
  3. A clean copy appears beside the original, without location, date or camera details.

With what macOS already has

In the Photos app, select a photo and choose Image → Location → Remove Location. That only covers photos in your Photos library and only the location.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

It works on any photo file in Finder and removes all hidden metadata at once: GPS location, capture date, camera and lens details. The image keeps its full resolution and orientation. It also removes location data from videos.

Questions

Does removing metadata reduce photo quality?

No. Where possible the image data is copied unchanged; only the hidden information is removed.

Will the photo still display the right way up?

Yes. The orientation tag is the one detail Bubbleshift keeps, so portrait photos stay upright.

Is the original changed?

No. You get a clean copy; the original keeps its metadata.
