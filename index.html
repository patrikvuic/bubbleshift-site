Drag. Shift. Convert.

A local file converter for Mac. Start dragging a file, tap ⇧ Shift, and drop it on the format you need. Tap ⌘ Command instead for one-tap actions.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

Convert mid-drag

No window to open. Tap Shift while you drag and a ring of formats appears under your pointer; the new file lands right beside the original, which stays untouched.

One-tap actions

Tap Command for actions: compress, merge PDFs, split pages, resize, remove location data, extract text, mute video or remove a background.

Sizes in a blink

Rest on Resize and it blooms into sizes: 50%, or a longest side of 1080, 2048 or 3840 pixels. Exact dimensions are a click away in the queue.

Or queue a whole batch

Drop files in the window, pick a format or an action for each, and convert them together. Images, documents, spreadsheets, PDFs and video.

Everything stays on your Mac

No uploads, no account, no tracking. Conversions, text recognition and background removal all run on your Mac.
