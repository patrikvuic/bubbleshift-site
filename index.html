Questions

What is Bubbleshift?

A file converter for Mac. Start dragging a file in Finder, tap Shift and drop it on the format you need; tap Command for actions like Compress, Merge PDFs, Collage, Resize, Rotate, Crop, Trim, Speed or Remove background. The new file appears beside the original.

Does Bubbleshift upload my files?

No. Every conversion runs on your Mac. There are no uploads, no account and no tracking, and it works offline.

Which formats can it convert?

Photos (HEIC, JPG, PNG, AVIF, WebP, RAW and more), PDFs, Word, Pages, Markdown, Excel, CSV, Numbers, Keynote and PowerPoint, videos (MOV, MP4, GIF), audio (M4A, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, MP3 input), subtitles and archives (ZIP, RAR, 7Z, TAR).

How much does it cost?

€4.99 once on the Mac App Store. No subscription and no in-app purchases.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon running macOS 13 Ventura or later. Background removal needs macOS 14.

Is it better than an online converter?

For private files, yes: nothing leaves your Mac, there are no file-size limits or watermarks, and it converts whole selections from Finder in one drag.
