⇧ Shift

Convert mid-drag

No window to open. Tap Shift while you drag and a ring of formats appears under your pointer; the new file lands right beside the original, which stays untouched.

⌘ Command

One-tap actions

Tap Command for actions: compress, merge PDFs, split pages, resize, crop, trim video, remove location data, extract text, mute video or remove a background.

Hold on Resize

Sizes in a blink

Rest on Resize and it blooms into sizes: 50%, or a longest side of 1080, 2048 or 3840 pixels. Or choose Custom and drag the image's corner to any size.

Main window

Or queue a whole batch
