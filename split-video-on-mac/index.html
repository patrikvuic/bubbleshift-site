How to split a video into two parts on a Mac

A long recording that should be two videos, or a clip that's too long to send in one piece: here is how to split it on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the video in Finder.
  2. Tap ⌘ Command while dragging and drop on Split.
  3. Pick the moment on the filmstrip, then click Split Here. Part 1 and part 2 appear beside the original.

With what macOS already has

QuickTime Player can trim but doesn't save two parts at once: duplicate the video and trim each copy (Edit → Trim).

Why use Bubbleshift for this

Both parts are written in one go, cut on the exact frame you chose, and the original stays as it is.

Questions

Is the cut exact?

Yes. It's frame-accurate.

Can I cut out just the middle?

Use Trim for that: it keeps the part between two handles.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Splitting happens on your Mac.
