How to reduce the file size of photos on a Mac

Upload forms, email and chat apps often reject large photos. Here is how to make them smaller on your Mac, one or many at a time.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the photo (or a whole selection) in Finder.
  2. Tap ⌘ Command while dragging and rest on Compress: it blooms into Low, Medium and High, each with the size it would come to.
  3. Drop on the one you want. The smaller copies appear beside the originals.

With what macOS already has

Open the photo in Preview, choose File → Export, pick JPEG and lower the Quality slider. In Finder, right-click a photo and choose Quick Actions → Convert Image, which can also make it smaller.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

You see each level's resulting size before choosing, it works on whole selections, and a copy is only kept when it's really smaller. JPEG and HEIC stay in their format; PNG photos become JPEG unless they have transparency.

Questions

Will the photos look worse?

Low barely changes how a photo looks; High saves the most space and can show softness up close.

Can I also make them smaller in pixels?

Yes. Use Resize for a set size such as 2048 pixels, or drag the corner to any size.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Compression happens on your Mac.
