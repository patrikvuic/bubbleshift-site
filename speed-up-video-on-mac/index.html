How to speed up or slow down a video on a Mac

Long screen recordings, timelapses or a slow-motion moment: here is how to change a video's speed on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the video in Finder.
  2. Tap ⌘ Command while dragging. Drop on Speed for 2×, or rest on it to choose 0.5×, 1.5×, 2× or 4×.
  3. The new video appears beside the original.

With what macOS already has

QuickTime Player can play faster but can't save a faster copy. In iMovie, select the clip in a project, click the speedometer button, choose a speed and export.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

One drag instead of a project, sound kept at its natural pitch, and the original untouched.

Questions

Does the sound sound like chipmunks at 2×?

No. The pitch is kept, so voices stay natural, just faster.

Can I make slow motion?

Yes. Choose 0.5× for half speed.

Is my video uploaded?

No. It's processed on your Mac.
