How to make a GIF from a video on a Mac

GIFs are handy for chats, bug reports and documentation. Here is how to turn a short clip or screen recording into a GIF on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the video (up to one minute) in Finder.
  2. Tap ⇧ Shift while dragging and drop on GIF.
  3. The GIF appears beside the video.

With what macOS already has

macOS has no built-in way to make a GIF from a video; Shortcuts can do it with a custom shortcut.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

No watermark, no upload and no account. GIFs are made at 12 frames per second and up to 640 pixels, a good balance between smoothness and size.

Questions

How long can the video be?

Up to one minute. Trim longer videos first with the Trim action.

Why is my GIF large?

GIF is an old, inefficient format. Shorter clips and smaller frames keep it small.

Is the video uploaded?

No. The GIF is made on your Mac.
