How to convert HEIC to JPG on a Mac

iPhones save photos as HEIC, which many websites, Windows PCs and older apps can't open. Here is how to turn them into JPG on your Mac, keeping them on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the HEIC photo (or a whole selection of them) in Finder.
  2. While still dragging, tap ⇧ Shift. A ring of formats appears under the pointer.
  3. Drop on JPG. The JPG copies appear right beside the originals, which stay untouched.

With what macOS already has

Open the photo in Preview, choose File → Export, pick JPEG as the format and save. For many photos, select them all in Preview's sidebar first, then export. In the Photos app, File → Export → Export Photos also lets you pick JPEG.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

It works straight from a Finder drag, for any number of photos, and keeps each photo's orientation, so pictures taken in portrait don't come out sideways. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Questions

Does converting HEIC to JPG lose quality?

JPG is a lossy format, so there is a small loss, but at Bubbleshift's quality setting the difference is not visible in normal use. If you need no loss at all, convert to PNG or TIFF instead.

Can I convert JPG back to HEIC?

Yes. Drag a JPG, tap Shift and drop on HEIC; HEIC files are usually about half the size.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The conversion runs entirely on your Mac.
