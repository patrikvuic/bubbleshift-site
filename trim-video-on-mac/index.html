How to trim a video on a Mac

Most clips have a slow start or an awkward ending. Here is how to keep just the part you want on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the video in Finder.
  2. Tap ⌘ Command while dragging and drop on Trim.
  3. Drag the two handles on the filmstrip (press Space to preview), then click Trim. The trimmed copy appears beside the original.

With what macOS already has

Open the video in QuickTime Player and choose Edit → Trim, then save. The cut snaps to keyframes in some videos.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

The cut is frame-accurate, the original stays untouched, and you never leave Finder for long.

Questions

Does trimming reduce quality?

The trimmed part is re-encoded at the highest quality so the cut can land on any frame.

Which formats work?

MOV and MP4 videos, and others macOS can play (saved as MP4).

Is my video uploaded?

No. Trimming happens on your Mac.
