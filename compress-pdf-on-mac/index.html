How to reduce the size of a PDF on a Mac

Scanned documents and PDFs full of photos are often too big for email or upload forms. Here is how to shrink them on your Mac without making the text blurry.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Start dragging the PDF in Finder.
  2. Tap ⌘ Command while dragging and drop on Compress.
  3. A smaller copy appears beside the original. If the PDF was already as small as it can get, Bubbleshift tells you instead of saving a copy.

With what macOS already has

Open the PDF in Preview, choose File → Export and set Quartz Filter to Reduce File Size. It is free, but it can reduce photos so much that they look blocky.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

It lowers the resolution of the photos inside the PDF to a sensible screen size (about 144 dpi) while text and drawings stay sharp and selectable, and it only keeps the result when it is meaningfully smaller.

Questions

Will the text still be searchable?

Yes. Only the images inside the PDF are re-compressed; the text layer is unchanged.

How much smaller will my PDF get?

It depends on the content. PDFs with large photos or scans often shrink by more than half; text-only PDFs barely change.

Can I compress images and videos too?

Yes. Compress works on JPEG, HEIC, PNG and other photos, and on videos.
