How to convert MOV to MP4 on a Mac

Many websites, editors and Windows PCs prefer MP4 over Apple's MOV. Here is how to convert one video or a whole batch on your Mac.

With Bubbleshift

  1. Select the MOV files in Finder and start dragging.
  2. Tap ⇧ Shift while dragging and drop on MP4.
  3. The MP4 copies appear beside the originals.

With what macOS already has

QuickTime Player can export to MOV only. iMovie can share as MP4, but you have to import the clip into a project first.

Why use Bubbleshift for this

When the video's codecs allow it, Bubbleshift re-wraps the video into MP4 without re-encoding, so it is nearly instant and keeps the exact quality. Otherwise it re-encodes at high quality.

Questions

Does converting MOV to MP4 reduce quality?

Usually not at all: most iPhone and camera videos can be re-wrapped without re-encoding.

Can I convert several videos at once?

Yes. Drag the whole selection and drop it on MP4.

Can it convert MP4 back to MOV?

Yes. Drag an MP4, tap Shift and drop on MOV.
