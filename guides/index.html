Guides

Common file jobs on a Mac, answered: with Bubbleshift, and with what macOS already has.

How to convert HEIC to JPG on a Mac

Turn iPhone HEIC photos into JPG on your Mac in seconds, one or many at a time, without uploading them to an online converter.

How to merge PDF files on a Mac

Combine several PDFs, or PDFs and images, into one PDF on your Mac and choose the page order, without uploading your documents.

How to make a photo collage on a Mac

Put several photos into one image on your Mac: side by side, stacked, in a grid or placed freely, at full resolution and without uploading them.

How to combine videos on a Mac

Join several video clips into one MOV or MP4 on your Mac, in the order you choose, without uploading them or opening a video editor.

How to reduce the file size of photos on a Mac

Make photos smaller on your Mac: pick Low, Medium or High compression and see the new size before you choose. JPEG, HEIC and PNG.

How to reduce the size of a PDF on a Mac

Make a large PDF small enough to email or upload, on your Mac, while keeping text sharp and selectable. No online upload needed.

How to remove location data from photos on a Mac

Strip GPS location, capture date and camera details from photos on your Mac before sharing them, while keeping the picture itself unchanged.

How to open RAR and 7Z files on a Mac

Extract RAR, 7Z, TAR.GZ and other archives on your Mac with a quick drag, or zip folders to share them.

How to convert RAW photos to JPG on a Mac

Turn camera RAW files (Canon CR3, Nikon NEF, Sony ARW, Fujifilm RAF, DNG) into JPG on your Mac with a single drag.

How to compress a video on a Mac

Make a large video small enough to send or upload, on your Mac, without an online compressor. Works with MOV and MP4 from iPhone or screen recordings.

How to convert MOV to MP4 on a Mac

Turn iPhone and QuickTime MOV videos into MP4 on your Mac, usually in seconds and without losing quality. No upload needed.

How to make a GIF from a video on a Mac

Make a GIF from a short video or screen recording on your Mac with one drag. No online GIF maker, no watermark.

How to resize images in bulk on a Mac

Resize one photo or a whole folder of images on your Mac in one drag: 50%, 1080, 2048 or 3840 pixels, keeping format and metadata.

How to convert WebP images to JPG on a Mac

Turn WebP images saved from websites into JPG or PNG on your Mac, so they open in every app. One drag, no upload.

How to convert a PDF to Word on a Mac

Get an editable Word document from a PDF on your Mac without uploading it. Keeps the text, fonts and sizes, one page per page.

How to extract the audio from a video on a Mac

Save just the sound of a video as an M4A audio file on your Mac: interviews, lectures, music. One drag, nothing uploaded.

How to remove a photo's background on a Mac

Cut out the subject of a photo as a transparent PNG on your Mac, in one drag. Private and offline, no online background remover.

How to copy text from an image or scan on a Mac

Extract the text from screenshots, photos and scanned PDFs on your Mac into a text file. Fast, private text recognition (OCR).

How to trim a video on a Mac

Cut the start and end of a video on your Mac with a filmstrip and two handles, frame-accurately. Keeps MOV or MP4.

How to crop a video to vertical 9:16 on a Mac

Turn a landscape video into a vertical 9:16 one on your Mac for Reels, TikTok or Shorts, or crop to 1:1, 4:3 or 16:9. No upload.

How to rotate a video on a Mac

Rotate a sideways video by 90° or 180°, or mirror it, on your Mac without re-encoding, so quality stays exactly the same.

How to speed up or slow down a video on a Mac

Make a video 1.5×, 2× or 4× faster, or half speed, on your Mac. The sound keeps its pitch. No video editor or upload needed.

How to split a video into two parts on a Mac

Cut a video into two separate files at the exact moment you choose, on your Mac, frame-accurately and without uploading it.

How to save a frame from a video as an image on a Mac

Grab a sharp still image from any moment of a video on your Mac, saved as a full-resolution PNG, instead of a blurry screenshot.

How to make an audio file louder on a Mac

Raise the volume of a quiet recording on your Mac without distortion: the loudest moment is brought up to just below clipping.

How to combine audio files on a Mac

Join several audio files or voice memos into one track on your Mac, in your order. WAV, AIFF and FLAC stay lossless.

How to make a scanned PDF searchable on a Mac

Add a hidden text layer to a scanned PDF on your Mac so you can search, select and copy its text. The scan looks exactly the same.
